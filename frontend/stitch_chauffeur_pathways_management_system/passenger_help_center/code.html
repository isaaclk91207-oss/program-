Frequently Asked Questions

How to request a car? expand_more
To request a car, navigate to the Home screen and tap the 'New Request' button. Fill in your destination, date, and purpose of travel, then submit for approval.
Tracking your assignment expand_more
You can track your assigned vehicle in the 'Trips' tab once your request has been approved by the Admin team.
Updating profile information expand_more
Go to the Profile tab and tap 'Edit Profile'. Here you can update your contact number, department, and default pick-up location.
Understanding the point system expand_more
Points are allocated monthly based on your department budget. Each trip deducts points based on distance and vehicle type requested.
