Privacy Policy

PCCP VECTOR - INTERNAL LOGISTICS

Data Handling & Privacy Guidelines

Last Updated: October 24, 2023

database 1. Data Collection

To facilitate internal corporate transport services, PCCP Vector collects specific operational data. This includes but is not limited to: employee names, corporate identification numbers (employee IDs), departmental affiliations, requested pickup and drop-off locations, and timestamps of transport requests.

insights 2. How We Use Information

The information collected is utilized strictly for operational efficiency and service improvement. Primary uses include optimizing vehicle routing, ensuring passenger safety and accountability during transit, and generating aggregate performance metrics to evaluate the efficacy of the corporate transport network.

share 3. Data Sharing

Data sharing is heavily restricted within the organization. Passenger transport data is shared exclusively with necessary internal logistics personnel, assigned drivers (limited to immediate trip execution details), and designated system administrators. We do not sell or share data with external third parties or public entities.

lock 4. Data Security

PCCP Vector employs enterprise-grade security measures to protect internal transport data. All collected information resides in encrypted storage environments. Access to raw data logs and passenger histories is governed by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), ensuring that only authorized personnel can view sensitive operational data.

contact_support 5. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy or the handling of your data within the PCCP Vector system, please contact the internal logistics administration team.
